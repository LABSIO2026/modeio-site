MODE.IO — Privacy Policy Effective date: [October 17, 2026] MODE.IO is developed by Manuele Gumina, publishing as LABS.IO This policy explains how the app handles your information. Summary MODE.IO does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data on servers we control. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. 1. Data you create in the app Your routines, profiles, activities, tasks, priorities, goals, counters, notes, voice recordings and photos are stored: • on your device; and • if iCloud is enabled for MODE.IO on your device, in the private CloudKit database of your own iCloud account, so it can sync between your devices. Your iCloud private database is accessible only to you. We cannot see, access or recover its contents. iCloud is provided by Apple and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). 2. Permissions • Camera — only to take photos you choose to attach to a daily note. • Microphone — only to record audio you choose to attach to a daily note. • Photo library — only to pick images you choose to attach to a note. • Notifications — only to deliver local reminders that you schedule (task and activity reminders, morning summary, streak and goal reminders, backup reminders). Notifications are generated on your device; no notification content is sent to us. You can revoke any permission at any time in the iOS Settings app. 3. Backups The app can export your data as a JSON file. The file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to save or share it. 4. No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking The app has no sign-up or login. It contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. 5. Data retention and deletion Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete all data from Settings › Delete all data inside the app. To remove synced data from iCloud, you can also go to iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › MODE.IO. Deleting the app removes local data from that device. 6. Children The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). 7. Your rights (GDPR and similar laws) Because we do not collect or process your personal data on our systems, we hold no data about you to access, correct, export or delete. All your data is under your control on your device and in your iCloud account. If you have questions, contact us at the address below. 8. Changes to this policy If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address. 9.
