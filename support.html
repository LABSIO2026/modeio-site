MODE.IO — Support Need help? Write to [SUPPORT_EMAIL]. We usually reply within a few days. FAQ • Do I need an account? No. MODE.IO works without sign-up. Sync uses the iCloud account already signed in on your iPhone or iPad. • My data doesn't sync between devices. Make sure both devices use the same Apple ID, iCloud Drive is on, and MODE.IO is enabled in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Then open the app on both devices while connected to the internet. You can check the iCloud status in MODE.IO › Settings. • Notifications don't arrive. Check Settings › Notifications › MODE.IO. Reminders are scheduled for the next 7 days: open the app at least once a week to keep them up to date. • How do I back up my data? MODE.IO › Settings › Backup › Export creates a JSON file you can save in Files or share. Use Import to restore it. • How do I delete everything? MODE.IO › Settings › Delete all data (triple confirmation). Privacy policy: [PRIVACY_URL]
